While the coast below hums with boats and crowds, Ravello sits serenely above it all, 350 metres up, wrapped in gardens and silence. This is the coast's most romantic address — a town of villas, music, and views so sweeping that composers and poets have been coming here for their inspiration for centuries.

Two legendary gardens

Villa Rufolo, with its Moorish cloister and flower-filled terrace, inspired Wagner and still hosts concerts on a stage that seems to float over the sea. A short walk away, Villa Cimbrone leads to the Terrace of Infinity, a stone balustrade lined with busts and open to a view that genuinely stops conversation.

The music

Each summer the Ravello Festival fills the town's gardens and terraces with classical music, jazz, and dance. Hearing an orchestra at sunset with the Gulf of Salerno shining below is one of those rare experiences that lives up to every expectation.

  • Villa Rufolo — gardens, concerts, and the famous cloister.
  • Villa Cimbrone — the Terrace of Infinity and shaded garden walks.
  • Piazza Centrale — a calm square for a slow coffee away from the coast's bustle.
  • The walk down to Amalfi — a steep, scenic path through lemon groves for the energetic.

Come up for the day by bus from Amalfi, or better, stay a night. When the last coach leaves and the gardens close, Ravello returns to the quiet that made it famous in the first place.

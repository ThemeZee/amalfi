Where you sleep on the Amalfi Coast shapes the whole trip — how early you reach the beaches, how late you linger over dinner, and how many stairs stand between you and your bed. From cliff-edge grand hotels to family-run B&Bs tucked down a lemon-scented lane, here is how to choose.

Grand hotels with a view

The coast's legendary hotels earn their reputation: infinity pools hung over the sea, terraces draped in wisteria, and service polished over generations. They are a splurge, but for a honeymoon or a milestone trip there is nothing like waking to that view. Book many months ahead for summer.

Cute boutique stays

Our favourite category. Small, characterful hotels — a dozen rooms, a shaded breakfast terrace, a host who tells you where the locals actually eat. Look in Praiano and Atrani, the quieter neighbours of Positano and Amalfi, where you get the same sea and a gentler pace for less.

Hidden B&Bs and lemon-grove rooms

  • Agerola & the hill towns — cool air, farm breakfasts, and a fraction of coastal prices.
  • Atrani — the coast's smallest town, with real neighbourhood life and an easy walk to Amalfi.
  • Sorrento — flat, well-connected, and full of mid-range rooms if you plan lots of day trips.

Ask one question before you book anything: how many steps from the road to the room? On this coast, it matters more than the star rating.

Whatever you choose, request a room facing the sea if the budget stretches to it. A morning coffee on a terrace above the Mediterranean is the kind of small luxury you will remember long after you have forgotten the price.

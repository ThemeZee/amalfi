If you only do one walk on the Amalfi Coast, make it this one. The Sentiero degli Dei — the Path of the Gods — runs high along the ridgeline between Bomerano and Nocelle, and for three unforgettable hours the entire coast unspools beneath your feet: terraced vineyards, sheer drops, and the islands of Capri shimmering on the horizon.

The route in brief

  • Distance: roughly 7.8 km one way
  • Time: 3 to 4 hours at a gentle pace, with plenty of photo stops
  • Difficulty: moderate — mostly downhill if you start in Bomerano
  • Best direction: Bomerano to Nocelle, so the views stay ahead of you and the climbing stays behind you

Starting out

Take the early bus from Amalfi up to Bomerano, in the hill town of Agerola. From the main square, follow the signs and the painted blue-and-white markers. Within twenty minutes the village falls away and the path opens onto the ridge, where the Mediterranean suddenly fills the whole southern sky.

The long descent

The trail ends above Nocelle, where roughly 1,500 steps drop you down to the road near Positano. Your knees will remember it, but the little bar at the top — with its cold drinks and its view straight down to the sea — is one of the finest rewards in walking.

Bring more water than you think you need, wear real shoes, and start before the midday heat. The path has almost no shade.

Go in spring and the hillsides are thick with wildflowers; go at golden hour and the whole coast turns to honey. Either way, you will understand at once why the ancients believed the gods walked here.

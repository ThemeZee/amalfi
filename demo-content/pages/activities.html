Ways to spend a day

A few things we never stop recommending.

Swim the hidden coves

Hire a little gozzo and drop anchor at the small beaches the ferries skip and the maps forget.

Walk the cliffside trails

Set out on the paths above the sea at first light, when the air is cool and the views go on forever.

Linger over long lunches

Settle in over fresh seafood and a carafe of local white at a table that looks straight out to sea.
